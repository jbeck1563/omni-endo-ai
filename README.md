# omni-endo-ai
A Docker-powered tool that lets you use your favourite AI (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, or any other) to analyse your OmniPod 5 and your glucose/bolus data. It securely pulls your records from Glooko and prepares a detailed "data-map" so you can get expert-level insights into your patterns and settings.
