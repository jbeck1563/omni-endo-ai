  1. Adjust the Audit Start/End dates below to match that window.

Glucose Timeline

Contents: Every CGM data point in the window plus 5-minute glucose velocity.

Use Case: Best for analyzing overnight stability and long-term basal efficacy.

Enriched Bolus Log

Contents: Bolus events matched with glucose at delivery, active ISF, CR, Target, and DIA.

Use Case: Identifying insulin stacking and verifying bolus calculator accuracy.

Hourly Trends

Contents: Aggregated TIR and Average Glucose distributed by clock-hour.

Use Case: Finding recurring circadian patterns like the Dawn Phenomenon.
